# The app's checks: stage, part, then the command (from the top).
# tests/app/run.sh runs them by stage, in order; ci/local.sh --full runs
# each part as one of its own (app:<part>), in its own copy of the built
# tree, at once: some edit sources in place and restore them. A part
# whose command takes `--list` (its cases, one a line) and then case
# names runs in up to CASE_LANES (6) slices of its cases, each a part of
# its own (app:<part>/<k>) in its own copy.
